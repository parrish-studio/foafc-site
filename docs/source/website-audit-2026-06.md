# Website Audit & Improvement Plan (June 13, 2026)

## Executive summary
I've taken over maintenance and development of our website, and this report lays out what I found and what I propose to do about it. The good news first: we have a genuinely solid foundation to build on. The site was recently built with Wix's new AI-assisted editor (Wix Harmony, released January 2026), the design is clean, our mission and vision come across well, hosting and the premium plan are paid through March 2028, and the plan already includes everything we need for events, forms, and supporter sign-ups at no extra cost.

Because the site was generated quickly with an AI builder, it launched with some sample content still in place. In practice it means a few of the pages visitors care about most aren't finished yet: the trail map and donate pages are empty, the online shop shows sample products instead of our real Bonfire merchandise, and a handful of buttons and social links don't yet point anywhere.

Priorities for the site: **(1) trail information, (2) events and community engagement, and (3) donations and supporter growth**, with merchandise handled simply by connecting the Bonfire store we already run. Phase 1 comes down to one idea: **make everything on the site real and current.**

## Platform notes
Wix Business plan, paid through March 2028: events and ticketing, forms, customer accounts, email marketing, e-commerce. Wix Harmony doesn't support custom application code (developer tooling is only in their other editors); small HTML snippets are supported. Custom functionality would mean rebuilding in Wix Studio.

## Phase 1 — Make everything real and current
1. **Publish the trail map** — full-width image readable on a phone, "Download printable map (PDF)" link, parking and directions notes.
2. **Finish the Donate page** — PayPal donate button (PayPal has a reduced processing rate for confirmed 501(c)(3)s), Venmo handle with QR code, mailing address for checks, legal name, EIN, tax-deductibility language. Repoint every Support Us / Donate Now button.
3. **Connect the real Bonfire store** — bonfire.com/friends-of-fierce-chase-merch (print-on-demand, USA-printed premium unisex tees, proceeds support the trails). Link out or embed.
4. **Make Events mirror Facebook** — embed the Facebook events/page feed and a "Follow us on Facebook" link. Facebook stays the single source of truth.
5. **Tidy copy and links** — tagline fixes, full site name in tabs and search, real Sign Up destination, fix Instagram link, remove YouTube and X icons (those accounts don't exist).
6. **Search and discovery** — page descriptions, alt text, Google Search Console, Google Business Profile.

## Phase 2 — Build the habit (before ski season)
- Surface Facebook content more prominently (recent posts on the homepage, event photos).
- Email sign-up: a "Get trail updates" form. Board to decide what the list is for and who manages it.
- Donation upgrade (board decision): evaluate Zeffy (0% platform fees) or Givebutter for recurring gifts and donor records.
- Analytics and a light performance pass.

## Phase 3 — Stretch goals
- **Trail conditions on the site.** John Chase already posts conditions via Facebook Live. Only pursue this if his Facebook updates can be mirrored automatically — never by asking him to post twice.
- Volunteer sign-up and trail-work coordination; landowner appreciation page; grant-ready content such as an annual impact summary.

## Maintenance and continuity
- A second board member with co-owner access to the hosting account and domain, with recovery details stored where the board can reach them.
- A one-page how-to for routine updates. Quarterly content review; renewal dates on the board calendar.
